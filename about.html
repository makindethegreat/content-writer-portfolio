05 · WHERE THIS COMES FROM

Apparently, I’ve always been like this.

HUMBLE BEGINNINGS

At 14, I had a strange little superpower: I could make things sound interesting.

I wrote essays on why we shouldn’t kill fireflies. I tried to convince people that cassava-flavoured ice cream could be the next big thing.

I wasn’t trying to become a marketer. I just liked ideas, stories, and getting other people to care about them.

That instinct followed me into journalism and communication. I got hooked on how information travels, how people decide, and why some messages stick while others disappear into the wind.

Then marketing. Then B2B SaaS, the wonderfully strange world where people build incredibly complicated products and then have to explain them to other humans.

That’s the part I love: take something complicated, find the interesting bit, connect it to something people already care about, and build systems that help the right people discover it.
