HOW THIS WHOLE THING STARTED

Apparently, I've always been like this.

HUMBLE BEGININGS

At around 14, I had a strange little superpower: I could make things sound interesting.

Sometimes, that meant writing essays about why we shouldn't kill fireflies. Other times, it meant trying to convince people that cassava-flavoured ice cream could be the next big thing.

Looking back, I probably wasn't trying to become a marketer. I just liked ideas, stories, and figuring out how to make other people care about them.

That instinct followed me into journalism and communication, where I became increasingly interested in how information travels, how people make decisions, and why some messages stick while others disappear into the wind.

Eventually, I found my way into marketing. And then into the wonderfully strange world of B2B SaaS—where people build incredibly complicated products and then have the difficult job of explaining them to other humans.

That's the part I enjoy: taking something complicated, finding the interesting bit, connecting it to something people already care about, and building systems that help the right people discover it.

SINCE THEN, I'VE EVOLVED. I BELIEVE THAT

Good content should do something.

Make a confusing idea clearer. Help a buyer make a decision. Create demand. Build trust. Start a useful conversation. Or occasionally make someone stop scrolling and think:

“Huh. That's actually interesting.”

That's the kind of work I strive to produce
